THE PLANNED EXPERIENCE

How connecting will work

  1. Account connection stays off

    No Riot account is collected or connected by this version.

  2. A private tracker is the goal

    We want each player to see only their own connected statistics, with no public profiles, player search, data sales or sharing with other visitors. This is a proposal, not an available service.

  3. Review before launch

    Riot must confirm that this private-only approach fits its requirements. Real login will remain disabled until the required approvals, backend, privacy notice and security checks are complete.

DATA & VISIBILITY

What data is handled now?

Current version: account linking disabled
DataCurrent handling
Riot credentials, identifiers, tokens and automatic match historyNot requested or collected by this version. There is no Riot login or account database.
Manual match notes and inventory checkmarksSaved in this browser on this device. This version does not upload them or make them public.
Website requestsYour browser requests website files from the hosting provider, which may process IP addresses and access logs under its own policies.

Shared devices: local notes are not an encrypted private account. Other people with access to your browser profile may be able to read them. Avoid entering personal or sensitive information.

Future connected data: the goal is to request only the minimum data needed for your personal tracker. No real collection will start based on this information page.

Your choices

You can clear manual match history using Clear log in the tracker. You can remove all Spider Stats browser data through your browser’s site-data settings; this also removes saved inventory checkmarks.

There are no connected accounts to disconnect in this version. Before a future integration launches, its data retention, deletion process, operator contact and actual data handling must be documented and reviewed.
